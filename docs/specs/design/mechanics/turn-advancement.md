# Overview

Time in the game moves only when the player ends a [Turn](../foundation/engine-contract.md#player-actions-and-turns). Advance turn is the [Player action](../foundation/engine-contract.md#player-actions-and-turns) that does so, under [Player actions](../foundation/engine-contract.md#player-actions) in Engine Contract, and everything that takes time happens in it at once, e.g. income, training, and [Combat](domain-model.md#combat).

Advance turn is a fixed sequence of twelve Turn advancement steps, from Agency income to Turn close, each applied exactly once and each applying the rules of one subject, e.g. in Upkeep the [Agency](domain-model.md#agency-and-agents) pays for every [Serving](agents-spec.md#agent-statuses) [Agent](domain-model.md#agency-and-agents). The sequence matters, because a step sees what the earlier steps of the same Advance turn did. Within one step the opposite holds: every rule uses Step-start values, apart from a few listed exceptions, so the order in which [Campaign instances](../foundation/core-concepts.md#campaign-instances) are processed never changes the result. Only the [World state](../foundation/engine-contract.md#player-actions-and-turns) after Advance turn is observable.

Each Mechanics specification says what its rules do; Turn Advancement says when each applies, in one place, so that the whole order can be read and checked at once.

# Glossary

Turn Advancement defines no terms of its own. Terms about [Turns](../foundation/engine-contract.md#player-actions-and-turns) and [Player actions](../foundation/engine-contract.md#player-actions) are in the [Engine Contract glossary](../foundation/engine-contract.md#glossary); [Turn advancement step](domain-model.md#turn-advancement-steps), [Step-start value](domain-model.md#turn-advancement-steps), and the game concepts the steps name are in the [Domain Model glossary](domain-model.md#glossary) and the glossaries of the Mechanics specifications that detail those concepts.

# Canonical order

Which Turn advancement step each rule applied during Advance turn belongs to, the order of the steps, and which values each step uses are stated canonically under [Turn advancement steps](#turn-advancement-steps) and [Values a step uses](#values-a-step-uses). The following rules are complete:

- **One step per rule:** Every rule that a specification states is applied during Advance turn belongs to exactly one Turn advancement step under [Turn advancement steps](#turn-advancement-steps). A rule that belongs to no step is not applied during Advance turn.
- **The canonical order prevails:** When another specification contradicts these sections about which step a rule belongs to, the order of steps, or which values a rule uses, that specification is defective and must be corrected to match them. Until it is corrected, these sections govern.
- **New rules:** A specification that adds a rule applied during Advance turn is complete only when [Turn advancement steps](#turn-advancement-steps) places the rule in an existing step or gains a step for it.
- **Steps are named:** Every other specification refers to a Turn advancement step by its name, e.g. "the Mission resolution step", never by its number in the order. Only Turn Advancement uses the numbers.
- **Restatements:** The description of each step restates rules that the owning Mechanics specification states in full. Where the two differ about what a rule computes or changes, rather than about when it applies, the owning Mechanics specification governs that content and the description is corrected.

# Turn advancement steps

**Proposed rule:** Advance turn applies the following Turn advancement steps, each exactly once, in this order. The list is exhaustive. "[At assignment](agents-spec.md#agent-statuses)" in the table has the meaning stated under [Transit across the steps](#transit-across-the-steps). Every step applies in every Advance turn, including the one in which the [Campaign](../foundation/core-concepts.md#campaign-and-session) receives its [Campaign ending](domain-model.md#campaign-standing); Advance turn itself is rejected once the Campaign has a Campaign ending, under [After a Campaign ending](panic-and-endings.md#after-a-campaign-ending) in Panic and Endings, so then no step applies.

## Turn number

**Proposed rule:** The turn number is the number of the current [Turn](../foundation/engine-contract.md#glossary), as a whole number of at least 1. The initial [Campaign state](../foundation/core-concepts.md#campaign-and-session) has turn number 1, and step 12 (Turn close) raises it by 1 in every Advance turn, so the [World state](../foundation/engine-contract.md#player-actions-and-turns) after the Advance turn that ends Turn t has turn number t + 1. No other rule changes the turn number.

## Transit across the steps

**Proposed rule:** Every step applies the [Transit rule](agents-spec.md#transit-rule) of Agents. Step 9, Previous turn transit arrivals, comes after every step in which an Agent works, fights, or recovers, so an Agent In transit stays In transit through all of them. The following statements are complete:

1. **At assignment in a step:** Wherever the rule of a step applies to Agents that are At assignment, it applies to an Agent exactly when that Agent is At assignment when the step begins, as a Step-start value. An Agent In transit when the Advance turn begins is still In transit in steps 1 to 8, and so is an Agent that became In transit in an earlier step of the same Advance turn, i.e. an Agent with a Forced return from Contracting in step 1, an Investigation member sent to Standby in step 4, and a survivor sent back, or a member of an interrupted Investigation sent to Standby, in step 5.
2. **Arrival:** An Agent that arrives in step 9 does nothing else in this Advance turn: it earns no income in step 1, gains no base skill in step 3, adds no Investigation progress in step 4, fights no Combat in step 5, and recovers neither current health nor exhaustion in step 8. Each of these steps comes before step 9, when the Agent is still In transit.
3. **Upkeep counts every Serving Agent:** Step 2 is not something an Agent does but a cost of the Agency, so it counts every Serving Agent as a Step-start value, including one In transit, one that arrives in step 9 of the same Advance turn, one on Recovery, and one that is Deployed, under [Upkeep](economy-and-upgrades.md#upkeep).
4. **Departure:** An Agent that becomes In transit during an Advance turn, in step 1, 4, or 5, does not arrive in step 9 of that Advance turn, because it was not In transit with that Assignment in the Turn-end state. It is still In transit in the World state after that Advance turn and arrives in step 9 of the next Advance turn.
5. **Destination ends during transit:** When the Assignment an Agent is In transit to ends because of something other than the Agent's own work, its Assignment still changes as the owning rule states. In scope this happens in step 4, when the other members' progress completes an Investigation, and in step 5, when an Investigation is interrupted because its Lead is not available: a member still In transit to it goes to Standby, In transit, with the rest of the [Current team](domain-model.md#leads-investigations-mission-sites-and-missions), under [Completion](investigations.md#completion) and [Lead no longer available](investigations.md#lead-no-longer-available) in Investigations. The redirect starts a new transit, so under statement 4 the Agent does not arrive in step 9 of that Advance turn and arrives at Standby in step 9 of the next Advance turn.

**Deployed Agents:** Deploying to a Mission site does not start In transit, and neither does moving an Agent between Standby and Training, under [Transit rule](agents-spec.md#transit-rule) in Agents. The `deployAgents` [Player action](../foundation/engine-contract.md#player-actions-and-turns) makes the selected Agents Deployed, At assignment, at once, under [Deploying Agents](missions.md#deploying-agents) in Missions, so in the next Advance turn they are At assignment when step 5 begins and fight in it. Every Mission Deployed in the Turn-end state is therefore resolved in step 5 of the first Advance turn after the deployment, and none waits for a later one. The way back takes transit: the survivors become In transit in step 5, recover nothing in step 8, do not arrive in step 9 of that Advance turn, arrive in step 9 of the next Advance turn, and can be assigned, or start recovering in step 8, only in the Advance turn after that one.

**Informative rationale:** With one Advance turn for every transit, an Agent given an order that starts In transit works for the first time in the second Advance turn after the order, e.g. an Agent assigned to Contracting during Turn 5 arrives in step 9 of the Advance turn ending Turn 5 and earns its first income in step 1 of the Advance turn ending Turn 6. Placing the arrivals after every other step that applies to Agents makes "an arriving Agent does nothing else" follow from the order alone, and the Turn-end state is read in one place, the arrivals step, to keep this Advance turn's departures In transit.

## Same-turn interactions

Because the steps run in one fixed order, something that happens early in an Advance turn can change what a later step of the same Advance turn does, e.g. a Faction Eliminated in Mission resolution no longer acts in Faction activity.

**Proposed rule:** The following statements are complete for the interactions between steps of the same Advance turn that no other specification states:

- **Mission sites created during Advance turn do not count down in it:** Only Mission sites that existed in the Turn-end state count down in step 6. An Initiative Mission site created in step 4 did not exist in the Turn-end state, and a Response Mission site created in step 7 does not exist yet when step 6 applies, so neither counts down in the Advance turn that creates it, under [Mission site expiration](missions.md#mission-site-expiration).
- **A Mission site created for a Eliminated Faction:** An Initiative Mission site created in step 4 whose target Faction is Eliminated, already or in step 5 of the same Advance turn, is Cancelled in the cancellation part of step 5, so no World state shows it Active, under [Cancellation](missions.md#cancellation). The same holds for any Mission site created in step 4 that cannot exist, under [Whether a Mission site can exist](missions.md#whether-a-mission-site-can-exist).
- **An Investigation whose Lead stops being available:** An Investigation that is Active after step 4 and whose Lead is not available after the cancellation part of step 5, e.g. because a Won Mission of step 5 made its Faction Eliminated, is interrupted in the interruption part of step 5, so no World state shows an Active Investigation of a Lead that is not available, under [Lead no longer available](investigations.md#lead-no-longer-available). It made its progress in step 4 of that Advance turn first, and that progress is lost.
- **Members of an interrupted Investigation do not recover in that Advance turn:** They become In transit in step 5, are In transit at step 8, and recover nothing; they do not arrive in step 9 of that Advance turn and arrive on Standby in step 9 of the next one.
- **A Deployed Mission is never cancelled:** Step 5 resolves every Deployed Mission before its cancellation part, which applies only to Active Mission sites, so every Deployed Mission becomes Won or Lost whatever else happens in that Advance turn, under [Cancellation](missions.md#cancellation).
- **Survivors do not recover in the Advance turn of their Combat:** A survivor of step 5 is In transit at step 8 and recovers nothing, under [Several changes in one Turn](agent-recovery.md#several-changes-in-one-turn) in Agent Recovery, and it does not arrive in step 9. The members of an Investigation completed in step 4 likewise recover no exhaustion in step 8.
- **Arrivals do not recover in the Advance turn of their arrival:** An Agent In transit to Recovery or Standby in the Turn-end state is still In transit at step 8 and recovers nothing; it arrives in step 9 and recovers from the next Advance turn on, under [Which Agents recover](agent-recovery.md#which-agents-recover).
- **Work exhaustion before recovery:** An Agent that works in step 1, 3, or 4 gains its work exhaustion there and, while still on its [Work Assignment](agents-spec.md#assignments), recovers nothing in step 8. An Agent with a Forced return from Training in step 3 is on Standby, At assignment, and recovers exhaustion in step 8 of the same Advance turn; one with a Forced return from Contracting in step 1 or from an Investigation in step 4 is In transit and recovers nothing, under [Several changes in one Turn](agent-recovery.md#several-changes-in-one-turn).
- **An Agent Killed in step 5:** It was Serving when step 2 began, so it was counted in Upkeep of this Advance turn, and it is counted in no later Advance turn. It stops counting toward the [Transport cap](economy-and-upgrades.md#glossary) in step 5.
- **A Faction Eliminated in step 4 or step 5:** It neither escalates nor carries out an operation in step 7 of the same Advance turn, under [Faction activity](factions.md#faction-activity). A Faction is made Eliminated in step 4 by the [Completion effect](leads-and-progression.md#completion-effect) of a Lead and in step 5 by a Won Mission.
- **Funding changes affect income from the next Advance turn:** Funding changes only in steps 5 and 6, which follow step 1, so a funding change first affects Agency income in the next Advance turn, under [Funding changes](economy-and-upgrades.md#funding-changes).
- **Panic changes apply once:** The Panic increases and Panic reductions of steps 5 and 6 are added to the Pending panic change and change Panic only in step 10, so Ending check in step 11 sees their clamped sum.
- **Turn close after a Campaign ending:** When step 11 records a Campaign ending, step 12 still applies: both Turn series of money are recorded and the turn number rises, so the Ending turn is one less than the turn number of the World state after that Advance turn, under [Recording the ending](panic-and-endings.md#recording-the-ending).

# Values a step uses

The result of Advance turn must not depend on how an implementation happens to order its work, so which value a rule reads, as it was before or after another rule changed it, is fixed.

The following rules are complete:

- **Earlier steps are visible:** A Turn advancement step uses every value as it is after every earlier step of the same Advance turn has been applied. E.g., Mission resolution uses each Agent's base skill after the Training step of the same Advance turn.
- **Step-start values within a step:** Every rule of one step uses Step-start values, unless [Exceptions to Step-start values](#exceptions-to-step-start-values) states an exception for that step. E.g., when two Agents gain base skill in Training, neither gain depends on the other, whichever Agent an implementation processes first.
- **Several changes to one value:** When more than one rule changes the same value within a step, or when contributions of several steps combine into one change, the combined result is stated under [Combined results](#combined-results).
- **[Campaign instances](../foundation/core-concepts.md#campaign-instances) created during Advance turn:** A Campaign instance created during a step takes part in a later step of the same Advance turn only when [Campaign instances created during Advance turn](#campaign-instances-created-during-advance-turn) states so for that later step.

The order in which an implementation processes the Campaign instances within one step is the Implementer's choice, because the rules above, together with the order in which an owning specification has one step create several Campaign instances, make its result the same for every order. Only the World state after Advance turn is observable, under [World state integrity](../foundation/engine-contract.md#world-state-integrity) in Engine Contract.

## Exceptions to Step-start values

A few rules need something other than a Step-start value: a value from before the Advance turn began, or a result that an earlier part of the same step has just produced.

**Proposed rule:** The following list of exceptions is exhaustive. Each one keeps the result independent of the order in which an implementation processes Campaign instances.

1. **Values of the Turn-end state:** Three rules read a value of the Turn-end state rather than a Step-start value: whether an Agent was In transit, and with which Assignment, in step 9, under [Transit across the steps](#transit-across-the-steps); whether a Mission site already existed, in step 6, under [Mission site expiration](missions.md#mission-site-expiration); and the money that Money at the end of turn records, in step 12, under [Turn series of money](economy-and-upgrades.md#turn-series-of-money).
2. **Ordered parts for one Campaign instance:** Where an owning specification states the rule of a step for one Campaign instance as parts applied in order, each part reads the results of the earlier parts for that same Campaign instance, and never the results of this step for another Campaign instance. The list of such rules is exhaustive:
   - In step 1, for the Agency and each Agent on Contracting: the income from the Step-start effective skills, then each such Agent's work exhaustion, then its Forced return from its new exhaustion, under [Agency income](economy-and-upgrades.md#agency-income) and [Work exhaustion](agents-spec.md#work-exhaustion).
   - In step 3, for one Agent on Training: its Training gain outcome and gain, then its work exhaustion, then its Forced return from its new exhaustion, under [Training growth](agents-spec.md#training-growth) and [Work exhaustion](agents-spec.md#work-exhaustion).
   - In step 4, for one Investigation: Investigation progress gain, then Assessment and the Completion check from the new Investigation progress, then completion, then each working member's work exhaustion, then the Forced returns from the new exhaustion with their Progress loss, and Abandonment when no member remains, under [Investigation progress](investigations.md#investigation-progress).
   - In step 5, for one Deployed Mission: its Combat, from the Step-start values of its [Combatants](domain-model.md#combat), then its lifecycle, its Killed Agents and survivors from the current health its Combat left, the survivors' Mission exhaustion from the exhaustion its Combat left, the survivors' Mission growth from its [Combat report](domain-model.md#combat) and the [Participation history](domain-model.md#agency-and-agents) that recorded it, and its [Consequences](domain-model.md#leads-investigations-mission-sites-and-missions), under [Resolving one Mission](missions.md#resolving-one-mission).
   - In step 6, for one Mission site: the countdown, then expiration when it reaches 0.
   - In step 7, for one Faction: escalation, then the operation part, which uses the Faction activity level the Faction reached by escalating earlier in the same step, both for its `operation_level_weights` and for its `operation_every_turns`, under [Faction activity](factions.md#faction-activity).
   - In step 8, for one Agent: health recovery, then the move to Standby when current health equals its effective max health; eligibility for exhaustion recovery is still decided by the Assignment when the step begins, under [Several changes in one Turn](agent-recovery.md#several-changes-in-one-turn).
3. **Cancellation and interruption in step 5:** The cancellation part of Mission resolution reads whether each Active Mission site can exist after every Deployed Mission of the step has been resolved and its Consequences applied, so a Faction made Eliminated by any Won Mission of the step counts, under [Cancellation](missions.md#cancellation). The interruption part then reads whether the Lead of each Active Investigation is available, after the cancellation part, under [Lead no longer available](investigations.md#lead-no-longer-available).

## Combined results

When several changes reach the same value within an Advance turn, the result must still be one well-defined value, whichever change an implementation applies first.

**Proposed rule:** The following combined results are complete:

- **Money:** In step 5, the money changes of all Missions resolved in the step add up; the sum does not depend on order and is never clamped, under [Consequences](missions.md#consequences). Steps 1 and 2 each make one change.
- **Funding:** The funding changes made in one step are summed and floored at 0 once, from the Step-start funding of that step, under [Funding changes](economy-and-upgrades.md#funding-changes). Steps 5 and 6 are separate steps, so each has its own sum and its own floor.
- **Panic:** Every Panic increase and Panic reduction of steps 1 to 9, which in scope come from steps 5 and 6, is added to the Pending panic change, and Panic changes only in step 10, by the whole sum, clamped once, under [Pending panic change](panic-and-endings.md#pending-panic-change).
- **Lead discovery:** Several Won Missions in step 5 can each make the `discovered_when` of a Lead hold; a Lead is discovered from the World state the Advance turn produces, so the result does not depend on order, under [Lead discovery](leads-and-progression.md#lead-discovery).
- **Eliminated:** Several Won Missions in step 5 that eliminate the same Faction make it Eliminated once, with one Eliminated turn, under [Elimination](factions.md#elimination).
- **Created Mission sites:** Several completions in step 4 and several Faction operation occurrences in step 7 each create their own Mission site, a distinct Campaign instance, under [Mission site creation](missions.md#mission-site-creation).

No Agent value is changed by the rules of two Campaign instances in one step: an Agent belongs to at most one [Current team](domain-model.md#leads-investigations-mission-sites-and-missions), and each step's rules for one Agent are those its owning specification states. Where one step changes an Agent's exhaustion twice, e.g. by its Combat and then by its Mission exhaustion in step 5, the changes are ordered parts under [Exceptions to Step-start values](#exceptions-to-step-start-values) and add up.

## Campaign instances created during Advance turn

**Proposed rule:** Mission sites and their Enemies are the only Campaign instances created during Advance turn; a Mission is created only by a Player action, during a Turn, and the following statements about them are complete:

- **An Initiative Mission site created in step 4** is Active from its creation. It takes part in the cancellation part of step 5, and in no other later step of the same Advance turn: it is not resolved in step 5, because it is not Deployed, and it does not count down in step 6, because it did not exist in the Turn-end state.
- **A Response Mission site created in step 7** takes part in no later step of the same Advance turn.
- **Enemies** created with a Mission site take part in no step of the Advance turn that creates them, because no Combat is fought at a Mission site created in that Advance turn: it has no Mission yet.

# Edge cases and failure behavior

- **Campaign ended in step 11:** Step 12 still applies, and both Turn series of money are recorded and the turn number rises; every later Advance turn is rejected, so no step applies again ([Same-turn interactions](#same-turn-interactions)).
- **Victory and Defeat in the same Advance turn:** A `win_campaign` Effect applied in step 4 and a Defeat condition that holds in step 11 give Victory, because Ending check tries Victory first, under [Ending check step](panic-and-endings.md#ending-check-step) ([Turn advancement steps](#turn-advancement-steps)).
- **Agent Killed in step 5:** It cost upkeep in step 2 of the same Advance turn and costs none from the next ([Same-turn interactions](#same-turn-interactions)).
- **Agent arriving in step 9:** It does nothing else in this Advance turn but is counted in Upkeep ([Transit across the steps](#transit-across-the-steps)).
- **Agent In transit to an Investigation that completes in step 4:** It adds no progress and goes to Standby, In transit, with the other members; it does not arrive in step 9 of this Advance turn and arrives at Standby in step 9 of the next one ([Transit across the steps](#transit-across-the-steps)).
- **Survivors of step 5:** They are In transit at step 8 and recover nothing, and they do not arrive in step 9; they arrive in step 9 of the next Advance turn and recover from the Advance turn after it ([Same-turn interactions](#same-turn-interactions)).
- **Agent leaving Recovery in step 8:** It becomes Standby, At assignment, and no later step of the same Advance turn applies to it; it can be assigned in the next Turn ([Turn advancement steps](#turn-advancement-steps)).
- **Mission site created in step 4 or step 7:** It does not count down in the same Advance turn ([Campaign instances created during Advance turn](#campaign-instances-created-during-advance-turn)).
- **Deployed on the last Turn before expiration:** Step 5 precedes step 6, so the team fights, its Mission is Won or Lost, and the Mission site is never Expired ([Transit across the steps](#transit-across-the-steps)).
- **Money negative after step 2 and positive again after step 5:** Ending check reads money after step 5, so a Won Mission's money in step 5 can prevent Defeat ([Values a step uses](#values-a-step-uses)).
- **Funding changes in steps 5 and 6 of one Advance turn:** Each step sums and floors its own changes ([Combined results](#combined-results)).
- **Nothing to apply to:** A Campaign with no Agent, no Investigation, no Mission site, and no Faction still applies all twelve steps; each does what the table states for that case, and step 12 always applies ([Turn advancement steps](#turn-advancement-steps)).
- **Advance turn after a Campaign ending:** Rejected; no step applies, under [After a Campaign ending](panic-and-endings.md#after-a-campaign-ending).
