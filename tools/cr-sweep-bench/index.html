CR Sweep Bench

Finds a Combat rating formula that tells the player when a team is safe. For every pairing of an Agent type and an Enemy team, it sweeps the Agent count and measures the win rate with the game's own engine, whatever the formula. A formula is then scored against those win rates without fighting anything again.

Starting the engine…

1. What to sweep

Enemy teams

More teams, one per line, as groups joined by +, e.g. boss and minions: 1 x s400 h60 d20 a40 + 12 x s40 h20 d8: s skill, h max health, d weapon damage, a armor.

Agent types

One per line, e.g. veteran, rifle and vest: s200 h30 d14 a20. A team is any number of Agents of one type, at full health and rested.

Rules

GDR values to fight with in place of the content's, one per line, e.g. exhaustion_per_defense = 0. Pairings under other rules are swept and kept apart.

The content's values

Sweep

For each pairing the Agent count is doubled until the team wins nearly every probe, the counts over which the win rate climbs are found, and the full number of Combats is fought at each of them.

2. Formula

Rating of one Combatant
Second number of one Combatant, summed as sum2; may be empty
Rating of a side
Rating of one Enemy, when it differs; may be empty
What an expression may read

A Combatant's expression reads skill, damage, health, armor, and count, the number of Combatants of its side. A side's reads sum, sum2, and count. Both read the parameters, the functions sqrt min max pow log exp abs ceil floor round, and the means of the other side, opp_count, opp_skill, opp_damage, opp_health, and opp_armor. A formula that reads the other side rates one team differently against each opponent, so use those only to find out what a formula misses.

Parameters

3. How the formula does

The safe ratio is the lowest rating ratio, Agents to Enemies, at or above which a team wins that often against every Enemy team: the highest ratio any pairing needs. Over-deployment is the Agents the safe ratio asks for, over the Agents a pairing truly needs. It is what a formula costs the player, and unlike the safe ratio it does not shrink when a rating is only rescaled, e.g. by showing its square root, so it is what Fit makes smallest and what formulas are best compared by.
