Combat Rating Bench

Every Mission site's Enemies fight Agent teams sized by a candidate Combat rating formula to a set share of the site's rating, e.g. 100%, 120%, and 150%. A good formula gives all the teams sized to one share the same win rate, whatever their skill and Upgrades and whatever that win rate is, e.g. 87% at 100%. Nothing is fought until you press a Run button; results are kept in the browser, and a cell whose team the current formula sizes differently shows its earlier result greyed until it is run again.

Setup

Agent teams are Agents of the AgentArchetype's health, weapon damage, and armor, at full health and rested, with the given base skill, plus the weapon damage and armor of an Upgrade set, e.g. 4/20 is +4 weapon damage and +20 armor, i.e. 2 Weapon damage Upgrades of +2 and 4 Armor Upgrades of +5. Each team takes the Agent count whose rating is closest to its share of the Mission site's rating. The Agent team is always the Agents' side, as in a Mission.

Retreat is off by default, by setting retreat_below_agent_rating_fraction to 0, because it reads the game's own Combat rating rather than this formula; when it is allowed, a retreat counts as a loss. A cell whose closest Agent count still misses its share by more than 5% is excluded: drawn dashed, never fought, and left out of the statistics and the fit, e.g. one Agent of skill 500 against a small site.

Formula

Each group of identical Combatants rates by its side's combatant expression, the Agent or the Enemy one; a team rates by the team expression over the sum of its Combatants' ratings. A Combatant that is not able to fight counts for nothing. The expressions are JavaScript, with Math in scope, e.g. sqrt(health).

Combatant types under the formula, and explanations

Combatant variables: skill (effective skill), base_skill, health, armor, damage, exhaustion, and the derived health_mult = 1 + (health − health_base) × cr_per_health, likewise armor_mult and damage_mult. An Agent's Weapon damage and Armor Upgrades count only as the weapon damage and armor they add. Agents are rated by the Agent expression and Enemies by the Enemy one; every preset sets both to the same expression. Team variables: sum, count (Combatants able to fight), and game (the game's base Combat rating, Σ effective skill). Every parameter, and every Combat value under Setup, is a variable of both.

combatant_cr is one Combatant's rating by the Agent or Enemy expression; × skill is that rating over its effective skill, i.e. how much its health, armor, and weapon damage raise or lower it. A team's rating then comes from the team expression over the sum of these, so with a team expression other than sum, e.g. sqrt(sum * count), a site's rating is on another scale than its Combatants'.

Unchecking Enabled switches that part of the formula off: its multiplier is 1, or team_exp is 1, whatever the values. Every change re-rates every Combatant, site, and Agent team at once, and Run fights the teams as the current formula sizes them. The Fit box only lets the Fit button change the parameter. Fit fights nothing: over the cells already fought for the current formula, it searches the checked parameters for the values whose ratings best predict the measured win rates, by the curve under Analysis, whose win rate at equal ratings the cells decide, and writes them into the table. The unchecked parameters stay as they are. The new values usually size many teams differently, so those cells turn grey until they are run again; run, fit, and run the grey cells until the values settle.

E.g. with skill * damage_mult and cr_per_damage 0.02, a site of CR 1,200 has been fought by 12 Agents of skill 100 without Upgrades, each rated 100, who won 50%, and by 10 Agents of skill 100 with 10 Weapon damage Upgrades, each rated 120, who won 85%. Both teams rate 1,200, yet their win rates differ by 35 points, so no curve fits both. Fit tries other values on the same two results: at 0.05 the second team rates 10 × 150 = 1,500, 1.25× the site, while the first team, without Upgrades, still rates 1,200, and a curve through 50% at 1× and 85% at 1.25× fits both. Fit writes 0.05. The team that now matches the site is 8 such Agents, not 10, so that cell turns grey until it is run again, and should then win about what the first team won.

Analysis

Over the cells with a result for the current formula, all within 5% of their share. The spread is the standard deviation of the win rates of one share; the dice alone, i.e. a finite number of Combats, leave the spread stated beside it, and the rest is the formula spread, the part a better formula can remove.

Win rates of each share

Win rate by rating ratio

The curve is P = 1 / (1 + e^−(k·ln r + b)) with r the Agent team's rating over the site's. Both k, how sharply the win rate turns, and b, which sets the win rate at equal ratings, are fitted to the cells, so the curve need not pass through 50%. Fit searches the checked parameters for the least squared error of this curve.
